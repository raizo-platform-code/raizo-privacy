RAIZO Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026
Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information RAIZO collects through the RAIZO mobile application and services (the "Service"), how it is used, and the choices available to you.

This Policy should be read together with the RAIZO Terms & Conditions. By using RAIZO, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

RAIZO is intended for users 18 years of age or older, consistent with the Terms & Conditions.

1. Information You Provide

When you register, sign in, or use RAIZO, you may provide information such as:

- an email/username and password, or a Google account (email, name, profile photo) when you sign in with Google;
- profile details such as display name, username, bio, gender, date of birth, and country;
- a profile picture or other images/media you choose to upload;
- content you submit through feedback, including optional screenshots.

You may also use RAIZO as a guest, in which case a temporary guest identifier is created instead of an email/password account.

2. Account and Authentication Information

To keep your account secure and let you stay signed in, RAIZO stores authentication tokens (access and refresh tokens) securely on your device using the operating system's secure storage.

If you sign in with Google, RAIZO receives basic profile information from Google (such as your email address, display name, and profile photo) and an identifier confirming that sign-in, which is used to create or access your RAIZO account.

Password-based accounts are authenticated through RAIZO's own backend; RAIZO does not have access to your Google password.

3. Device Information

RAIZO generates a random device identifier that is stored securely on your device and sent with backend requests. This identifier is created locally by the app itself — it is not your phone's hardware ID, IMEI, or advertising ID — and it is used to help enforce device-level restrictions (for example, when a device has been restricted for abuse or policy violations).

Reinstalling the app or clearing app data resets this identifier.

4. Audio Room and Service Activity

RAIZO provides live audio rooms using a real-time voice transport provider (Agora) to carry the audio between participants while a room is active. Microphone access is requested only when needed to join or speak in an audio room.

RAIZO also processes activity related to your use of the Service, such as rooms you create or join, seats, moderation actions, and similar room-related events, in order to operate those features.

5. Messages and Interactions

Where RAIZO provides social or communication features — such as room chat, reactions, following/followers, blocking, profile visits, and reports — the associated content and activity is processed by RAIZO's backend in order to provide and moderate those features.

6. Uploaded Content

Images and other media you choose to upload — such as a profile picture, an audio-room cover image, feed post media, or feedback screenshots — are uploaded to and stored by RAIZO's own backend storage, not a separate third-party cloud storage provider.

7. Coins, Gifts, and Transaction Records

Where RAIZO provides virtual coins, gifts, recharge, or similar paid or reward features, records associated with your account (such as balances and transaction history) are processed and stored by RAIZO's backend in order to operate those features.

As described in the Terms & Conditions, payments may be handled by RAIZO or by an authorized third-party payment provider; this Policy does not claim a specific payment provider or describe how that provider separately handles payment details, since that depends on the payment method you use.

8. How We Use Information

Information described in this Policy is used to:

- create, secure, and maintain your account;
- operate core Service features (audio rooms, chat, gifts, coins, games, and related functionality);
- authenticate you and keep your session working across app restarts;
- enforce these Terms and this Policy, including responding to reports and abuse;
- deliver push notifications relevant to your account and activity;
- respond to feedback you submit;
- maintain the security, stability, and integrity of the Service.

9. Safety, Moderation, and Abuse Prevention

RAIZO may use account, device, and activity information described in this Policy to detect, investigate, and respond to fraud, platform abuse, and violations of the Terms & Conditions — including actions such as room moderation, blocking, reporting, and account or device restrictions described in the Terms.

10. Service Operation and Security

RAIZO relies on its backend infrastructure to operate the Service, including authenticating requests, storing account and activity data, and maintaining server-side authoritative records as described in the Terms & Conditions.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and RAIZO cannot guarantee absolute security. This Policy does not claim a specific encryption standard or security certification beyond what is described here.

11. Third-Party Services Actually Used by RAIZO

RAIZO currently relies on a small number of third-party services to operate specific features:

- Agora — real-time voice transport for live audio rooms;
- Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method, used only if you choose to sign in with Google;
- Firebase Cloud Messaging — used only to deliver push notifications to your device; it is not used for analytics, crash reporting, or advertising.

RAIZO does not currently integrate any analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising/tracking SDK in the app.

These providers process the information necessary to provide their respective function and are subject to their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

12. Data Retention

RAIZO keeps your account and activity information while your account exists, so the Service can work for you.

When you delete your account (see Section 14), your personal information is erased when deletion completes — immediately for guest accounts, after 7 days for registered accounts. Only records needed for financial, fraud-prevention, safety and legal purposes (such as coin, gift and payment transactions, moderation records and audit logs) are kept, linked to an anonymous "Deleted ID" and not to your name, photo or contact details, for as long as those purposes require.

Live audio-room voice is carried in real time and is not recorded or stored by RAIZO. Voice messages you choose to send in private chat are stored like other messages.

13. Your Choices and Account Controls

You can review and update certain profile information (such as display name, bio, and profile picture) directly within the app.

You can control visibility-related settings, block other users, and manage saved accounts on your device through the app's existing account and privacy settings.

You can decline microphone access, but doing so will prevent you from speaking in audio rooms.

14. Account Deletion

Removing a saved account from the device's account switcher only removes it from that device's local list — it does not delete your underlying RAIZO account or data.

You can delete your account yourself in the app: Profile > Settings > Delete Account. You do not need to contact support. The app asks you to type DELETE to confirm, and nothing is treated as done until the server confirms it.

Guest accounts (not linked to an email, phone number, password or Google sign-in) are deleted immediately after you confirm. This cannot be undone.

Registered accounts, including hosts and agency owners, are locked as soon as you confirm and are permanently deleted automatically after 7 days. While locked you cannot enter rooms, chat, send or receive gifts, or earn rewards. The app shows the time remaining. You can cancel and restore the account at any time before the 7 days end. Signing in again does not cancel deletion — you must choose to restore the account.

What is deleted: your profile (name, photo, bio, gender, birthday), email and phone number, password and sign-in sessions, followers and following, friends, blocks, profile visitors and CP relationships, feed posts, comments and likes, messages you sent (others see them as deleted), notifications and push tokens, inventory and equipped items, and your appearance in rankings and leaderboards. Hosts are removed from their agency and lose hosting privileges and future rewards. When an agency owner's account is deleted, the agency is closed and every host is released; hosts keep their own accounts and balances and can join another agency.

What is kept: records needed for financial, tax, fraud-prevention, dispute and legal purposes — such as coin, diamond, gift, recharge, salary and commission transactions, moderation records and audit logs. These are linked only to an anonymous "Deleted ID" record, not to your name, photo or contact details.

Balances: when deletion completes, remaining coins are returned to RAIZO, diamonds and frames/items are removed, and approved salaries or commissions that are not yet paid are cancelled. None of these are refunded or paid later, even if you create a new account. Restoring the account during the 7 days keeps everything unchanged.

If you cannot use the app, you can delete your account on the web page linked from https://raizo-platform-code.github.io/raizo-privacy/delete-account/ (sign in with your password or Google account), or email raizo.support@gmail.com with your RAIZO user ID or sign-in email. Never send passwords, one-time codes, tokens or payment details. Ownership must be verified before an emailed request is carried out. Email does not automatically create or cancel an in-app request.

RAIZO does not knowingly collect information from individuals under 18. If you believe a user under 18 has provided information to RAIZO, please contact RAIZO through the in-app Feedback feature so appropriate action can be taken.
