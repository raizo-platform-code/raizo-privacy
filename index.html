RAIZO Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 31, 2026
Last Updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information RAIZO collects through the RAIZO mobile application and services, how it is used, and the choices available to you. This Policy should be read together with the RAIZO Terms & Conditions. By using RAIZO, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. RAIZO is intended for users 18 years of age or older.

1. Information You Provide

When you register, sign in, or use RAIZO, you may provide: an email/username and password, or a Google account when you sign in with Google; profile details such as display name, username, bio, gender, date of birth, and country; a profile picture or other images/media you choose to upload; content you submit through feedback. You may also use RAIZO as a guest.

2. Account and Authentication Information

RAIZO stores authentication tokens securely on your device. If you sign in with Google, RAIZO receives basic profile information from Google. RAIZO does not have access to your Google password.

3. Device Information

RAIZO generates a random device identifier stored securely on your device. This is not your phone's hardware ID, IMEI, or advertising ID. Reinstalling the app resets this identifier.

4. Audio Room and Service Activity

RAIZO provides live audio rooms using Agora for real-time voice. Microphone access is requested only when needed to join or speak in a room.

5. Messages and Interactions

Room chat, reactions, following/followers, blocking, profile visits, and reports are processed by RAIZO's backend to provide and moderate those features.

6. Uploaded Content

Images and media you upload are stored by RAIZO's own backend storage.

7. Coins, Gifts, and Transaction Records

Balances and transaction history are processed and stored by RAIZO's backend. Payments may be handled by RAIZO or an authorized third-party payment provider.

8. How We Use Information

To create and secure your account; operate core features; authenticate you; enforce Terms and Policy; deliver push notifications; respond to feedback; maintain security and stability of the Service.

9. Safety, Moderation, and Abuse Prevention

RAIZO may use account, device, and activity information to detect and respond to fraud, abuse, and policy violations.

10. Service Operation and Security

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. RAIZO cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-Party Services

  • Agora — real-time voice for audio rooms
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications only

RAIZO does not integrate any analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising/tracking SDK.

12. Data Retention

RAIZO retains information as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service.

13. Your Choices and Account Controls

You can update profile info within the app. You can block users and manage saved accounts. Declining microphone access will prevent speaking in audio rooms.

14. Account Data Removal

A dedicated in-app account-deletion feature is not available in the current version. You may reach RAIZO through the in-app Feedback feature or email raizo.support@gmail.com to request assistance with account data removal.

15. Children's Privacy

RAIZO is intended only for users 18 years of age or older. RAIZO does not knowingly collect information from individuals under 18.

16. Changes to This Policy

RAIZO may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date identifies the current version.

17. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, feedback, or privacy-related requests regarding RAIZO, please email raizo.support@gmail.com. You may also use the in-app Feedback feature.
