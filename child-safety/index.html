RAIZO child safety standards

RAIZO (developer: RAIZO TEAM) has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page describes the standards that apply to everyone who uses the RAIZO app.

1. Who can use RAIZO

RAIZO is only for people aged 18 or older. Accounts that we believe belong to a person under 18 are removed.

2. What is prohibited

It is strictly prohibited to use RAIZO — including voice rooms, room chat, private messages, profile photos, feed posts and comments — to:

  • create, upload, share, request or link to any sexual content involving a minor (CSAM);
  • groom, sexualise, solicit, threaten or exploit a minor in any way;
  • arrange to meet a minor for sexual purposes, or traffic or sell a child;
  • share content that promotes or normalises the sexual abuse of children.

3. How to report

Inside the app you can report a user or content and block any user at any time. You can also email raizo.support@gmail.com with the RAIZO user ID, room or post concerned and a short description. Do not send copies of abusive images — describe where they are instead.

4. What we do

  • Reports about child safety are reviewed with the highest priority.
  • Content that breaks these standards is removed, and the accounts involved are permanently banned.
  • Where we become aware of CSAM, we report it to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement in the user's country, and preserve information as the law requires.
  • We comply with applicable child safety laws.

5. Child safety contact

Child safety point of contact: RAIZO TEAM — raizo.support@gmail.com
