Privacy Policy · Account deletion

RAIZO account deletion

Use this page to find the available way to request deletion of your RAIZO account and associated personal data. This static page never collects passwords or records requests.

Open the secure RAIZO deletion-request service

Official account-deletion support: raizo.support@gmail.com. You can email a request even if you cannot sign in. Include your RAIZO user ID or sign-in email if known. Never send passwords, one-time codes, tokens or payment details. Ownership must be verified before deletion. Email does not automatically create or cancel an in-app request and does not confirm completed deletion.

From the RAIZO app

  1. Open Profile, then Settings, then Delete Account.
  2. Read what will be deleted and kept, type DELETE and confirm.
  3. Guest accounts are deleted immediately. Registered accounts (including hosts and agency owners) are locked and permanently deleted after 7 days.

Cancel and restore

During the 7 days, open Delete Account (or sign in — the app shows a restore screen) and choose Cancel deletion and restore account. Signing in alone does not cancel deletion. After the 7 days, deletion is automatic and cannot be undone.

Can't use the app?

Use the secure deletion service linked above (password or Google sign-in). Or use the official support email above. Do not create another account to request deletion of the original.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted: profile, photo, contact details, password and sessions, followers, friends, blocks, visitors, CP, feed posts, sent messages, notifications, items, and your place in rankings. Hosts leave their agency and lose future rewards; when an agency owner is deleted the agency closes and its hosts are released with their own accounts and balances. Kept: financial, tax, fraud-prevention and audit records (coins, diamonds, gifts, recharges, salaries, commissions, moderation), linked only to an anonymous "Deleted ID". When deletion completes, remaining coins go back to RAIZO, diamonds and frames are removed, and unpaid salaries or commissions are cancelled — nothing is refunded or paid later. Restoring within the 7 days keeps everything.
